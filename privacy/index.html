Privacy Policy

Obstacool — Last updated: May 27, 2025

Developer: emesbeeuy  |  App: Obstacool  |  Platform: iOS

This Privacy Policy describes how the Obstacool mobile application ("the App", "we", "our") handles information when you use it. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

Information We Collect

The App itself does not run any servers or databases, and does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information to us directly.

The following data is stored locally on your device only:

  • Your high score and game statistics (streak, loss count)
  • Daily challenge completion status
  • Whether you have purchased the ad-free upgrade

This data never leaves your device and is managed by your iOS system storage.

Advertising and Tracking (AdMob)

The free version of the App displays advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) and other information to serve personalized ads.

On first launch the App requests your permission to track via Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework. If you decline, AdMob will serve non-personalized ads instead.

You can change your tracking preference at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Purchasing the ad-free upgrade removes all advertisements and stops AdMob from being initialized.

In-App Purchases

The App offers optional in-app purchases (remove ads / premium license). All payment processing is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive, store, or process any payment or billing information. Please review Apple's Privacy Policy for details.

Game Center

The App integrates with Apple Game Center to submit your high score to global leaderboards. This integration uses your Game Center identity as managed by Apple. We do not store any Game Center data on our own servers. Please review Apple's Privacy Policy for details on how Game Center handles your data.

Social Sharing

The App includes optional buttons to share your score or invite friends. These actions use iOS's native share sheet and are entirely opt-in. No data is shared without you explicitly tapping a share button.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob — Privacy Policy
  • Apple Game Center — Privacy Policy
  • Apple In-App Purchase — Privacy Policy

Changes to This Policy
